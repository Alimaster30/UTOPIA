The Tilton Story

Tilton fosters the development of skills — in all aspects of boarding school life — that prepare students to embrace and navigate a world driven by diversity and change.
Our community is built on human relationships that enable us to know, support, and challenge our students as we learn to do hard things well.
Tilton School creates opportunities that prepare students to make a difference in the world. Earning a diploma signifies that the student’s body of work demonstrates an array of knowledge, skills and understanding necessary to succeed in the 21st century world and in a rigorous college program.

Our Community

Our community is made up of students, faculty, and staff who care for and learn from one another. Together, we craft opportunities that you can’t find anywhere else.

Play fearlessly embraces the new

Play engages the world for curiosity’s sake, for the purpose of trying something new, without fearing the consequences. Play is our word for pure research, for raw experimentation, and for harmless trial and error.

Inquiry builds understanding

Inquiry looks beyond easy formulations of a problem and easy solutions. It seeks out multiple data types, looks at things from multiple angles, and thinks critically about the role bias in the interpretation of facts and the formulation of opinions.
